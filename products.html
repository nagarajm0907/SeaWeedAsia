Our Products

Five species from Indonesian waters, each one traceable back to the farm bed it grew on.

Cottonii — Eucheuma cottonii

Our highest-volume species, grown on long-lines and prized for its kappa carrageenan yield. We dry it to a consistent 35–38% moisture and hand-sort every batch before it's baled.

MOQ: 1 container

Spinosum — Eucheuma spinosum

Grown alongside cottonii but processed separately for buyers who need iota carrageenan's softer, more elastic gel.

MOQ: 1 container

Gracilaria — Gracilaria spp.

Farmed in brackish ponds rather than open sea, resulting in a cleaner, lower-ash harvest for agar production.

MOQ: 1 container

Sea Lettuce — Ulva lactuca

Wild-harvested from reef flats and shade-dried instead of sun-dried to hold on to its colour and mineral content.

MOQ: 1 container

Sargassum — Sargassum spp.

Wild-collected along our sourcing coastline and processed for alginate buyers and biostimulant manufacturers.

MOQ: 1 container
