Cottonii — Eucheuma cottonii

MOQ: 1 container

Cottonii is our highest-volume species. Indonesian farmers have grown this thick, cartilaginous red seaweed for generations — it's fast-growing and reliable, and its high kappa-carrageenan content makes it the standard raw material for gelling and thickening applications worldwide. It's also our first species onboarded onto SeaweedAsia's farm-to-batch traceability system.

Key Advantages

  • Kappa-carrageenan yield strong enough for firm-gel applications
  • Fast-growing, with a predictable harvest cycle of roughly 45 days
  • Holds up well through drying, baling, and long-haul shipping
  • Can be processed as whole dried leaf, chips, or milled powder depending on buyer spec

Product Specifications

Moisture35–38% at time of packing
Foreign matterCapped at 3–5%
Drying methodSun-dried
ColourYellow-green through to light brown depending on harvest stage
Standard pack50 kg woven bags; custom sizing on request

Cultivation & Processing

Farmers tie seedlings onto long-line ropes anchored just below the surface, where moving currents keep oxygen levels high. After six to seven weeks at sea, mature thalli are cut, rinsed of sand and debris, then spread on raised bamboo racks to dry in open air — never machine-dried, which keeps the fibre and colour intact.

Applications

  • Food & beverage: gelling and thickening agent in dairy, confectionery, sauces, and processed meat
  • Personal care: base ingredient in masks, scrubs, and moisture-retaining formulations
  • Nutraceutical: functional additive in select health products
  • Emerging uses: livestock feed supplement, organic soil amendment, early-stage bioplastic and biofuel trials

Spinosum — Eucheuma spinosum

MOQ: 1 container

Spinosum grows in the same waters as cottonii but yields a different compound — iota-carrageenan, known for a softer, more elastic set than cottonii's firmer gel. We source it for buyers who specifically need that texture, from the same farmer network we already work with.

Key Advantages

  • Elastic, soft-set gel profile suited to dairy and confectionery work
  • Tolerant of a wide salinity range, which keeps supply steady across seasons
  • Long-line farming method keeps quality consistent from farm to farm
  • Reliable harvest rhythm once the lines are properly maintained

Product Specifications

Moisture12% or below
Foreign matterKept minimal through hand-sorting
Drying methodSun-dried
ColourLight tan to yellowish-brown
Standard pack25–50 kg bales

Cultivation & Processing

Like cottonii, spinosum is tied onto long-lines and left to grow with the current — usually 40 to 45 days before it's ready. Once harvested, it goes through the same rinse-and-dry routine on our partner farms before consolidation at the warehouse.

Applications

  • Food & beverage: yoghurt, milk-based desserts, soft-set gels, general thickening
  • Personal care: lotions, creams, and hair-care formulations
  • Nutraceutical: gel-capsule and controlled-texture health products
  • Other: pet food, industrial stabilisers, ongoing biodegradable-packaging trials
Agar grade

Gracilaria — Gracilaria spp.

MOQ: 1 container

Gracilaria is our agar species, farmed in brackish ponds rather than open sea. Pond cultivation means farmers have more control over water quality than open-sea farming allows, and it's the raw material behind agar-agar — used everywhere from lab petri dishes to plant-based desserts.

Key Advantages

  • Strong agar yield per kilogram of dried biomass
  • Pond cultivation gives farmers more control over salinity and water quality than open-sea farming
  • Can be harvested several times a year rather than one seasonal cycle
  • Cleaner raw material, with lower ash content after processing

Product Specifications

MoistureTypically under 12%
Foreign matterMinimal
DryingSun-dried under hygienic, covered conditions
Texture / colourCoarse strands, brownish-red
Standard packCompressed bales

Cultivation & Processing

Farmers manage gracilaria in shallow earthen or lined ponds, adjusting water flow and salinity by hand to keep growth steady. Harvested strands are rinsed thoroughly before drying — the pond environment means less sand and debris to deal with compared to open-sea species.

Applications

  • Food & beverage: agar base for desserts, jellies, and plant-based food formulation
  • Personal care: thickening and moisture-binding agent
  • Pharmaceutical: capsule shells, controlled-release formulations
  • Other: laboratory culture media, organic soil inputs, early-stage bio-material development

Sea Lettuce — Ulva lactuca

MOQ: 1 container

Sea lettuce isn't farmed — it grows wild across Indonesia's reef flats, and we harvest it where it naturally thrives. Its bright colour and mineral density have made it a rising ingredient in health-food circles.

Key Advantages

  • Naturally high in minerals, a strong selling point for the health-food segment
  • Shade-drying, instead of full sun, keeps colour and nutrients from degrading
  • No cultivation input required, since it's wild-harvested

Product Specifications

DryingShade-dried, not sun-dried
FormWhole-leaf, dried — can be milled or flaked further on request
PackagingBags or bales, sized to order

Cultivation & Processing

Collected from reef flats at low tide, sea lettuce is rinsed to remove sand and other marine growth, then dried under shade rather than direct sun — a slower process, but one that keeps its colour and nutrient profile intact.

Applications

  • Food & beverage: health-food ingredient, salads, snacks, seasoning blends
  • Personal care: natural extract for skincare formulations
  • Other: animal feed additive, soil-conditioning trials
Alginate grade

Sargassum — Sargassum spp.

MOQ: 1 container

Sargassum is a wild brown seaweed we collect rather than cultivate, gathered from coastal waters near our farming communities. It's our alginate species — and increasingly, a raw material buyers ask about for biostimulant and agricultural use.

Key Advantages

  • Alginate content suited to industrial extraction
  • Naturally abundant, which keeps farming input costs low
  • Growing demand from the biostimulant and eco-product sector
  • Flexible processing — whole dried, powdered, or extracted

Product Specifications

Moisture15% or below
Foreign matterMinimal after cleaning
Drying methodSun-dried
ColourDark brown to olive
Standard pack25–50 kg compressed bales

Cultivation & Processing

Because it's wild-collected rather than farmed, sargassum goes straight from coastal waters into our cleaning process — sand and shell fragments removed, washed, sun-dried, sorted by size, then baled for export.

Applications

  • Food & beverage: alginate-based thickeners and stabilisers
  • Personal care: anti-ageing and moisture-retaining formulations
  • Pharmaceutical: bioactive compounds for supplements and drug development
  • Agriculture: organic fertiliser, soil conditioner, and a growing biostimulant market
