Quality Assurance

From Ocean Farming to Global Shipping

How we maintain stringent quality control across every stage of the supply chain.

01

Ocean Planting & Harvesting

Cultivated along 45-day growth cycles on monofilament long-lines in pristine coastal currents. Harvested by hand to preserve crop health and carrageenan integrity.

02

Solar Platform Drying

Dried exclusively on elevated bamboo tables above coastal sands to prevent silica contamination and ensure moisture drops below 35% evenly.

03

Sorting & Quality Testing

Manual sorting tables remove residual salt, shells, and non-target species. Batches are sample-tested for gel strength and anhydrous yield.

04

Baling & Container Dispatch

Hydraulically compressed into high-density 100kg bales, moisture-sealed with protective liners, and loaded into 40ft High Cube containers for international export.
