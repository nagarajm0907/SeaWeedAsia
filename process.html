Our Process

  1. Farm: Grown on racks or long-lines suited to each species, on farms we've mapped and registered by GPS.
  2. Harvest: Graded by our field team at the point of harvest, tagged with a batch ID tied to that farmer and plot.
  3. Dry: Sun-dried on raised bamboo racks — never machine-dried, preserving colour and gel strength.
  4. Verify: Second inspection and lab testing at our consolidation point, checked against the batch record.
  5. Ship: Container-ready packaging, with every bag traceable back to the farmer and farm plot.
