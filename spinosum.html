Spinosum — Eucheuma spinosum

Spinosum grows in the same waters as cottonii but yields a different compound — iota-carrageenan, known for a softer, more elastic set than cottonii's firmer gel. We source it for buyers who specifically need that texture, from the same farmer network we already work with.

Key Advantages

  • Elastic, soft-set gel profile suited to dairy and confectionery work
  • Tolerant of a wide salinity range, which keeps supply steady across seasons
  • Long-line farming method keeps quality consistent from farm to farm
  • Reliable harvest rhythm once the lines are properly maintained

Product Specifications

Cultivation & Processing

Like cottonii, spinosum is tied onto long-lines and left to grow with the current — usually 40 to 45 days before it's ready. Once harvested, it goes through the same rinse-and-dry routine on our partner farms before consolidation at the warehouse.

Applications

  • Food & beverage: yoghurt, milk-based desserts, soft-set gels, general thickening
  • Personal care: lotions, creams, and hair-care formulations
  • Nutraceutical: gel-capsule and controlled-texture health products
  • Other: pet food, industrial stabilisers, ongoing biodegradable-packaging trials
