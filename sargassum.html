Sargassum — Sargassum spp.

Sargassum is a wild brown seaweed we collect rather than cultivate, gathered from coastal waters near our farming communities. It's our alginate species — and increasingly, a raw material buyers ask about for biostimulant and agricultural use.

Key Advantages

  • Alginate content suited to industrial extraction
  • Naturally abundant, which keeps farming input costs low
  • Growing demand from the biostimulant and eco-product sector
  • Flexible processing — whole dried, powdered, or extracted

Cultivation & Processing

Because it's wild-collected rather than farmed, sargassum goes straight from coastal waters into our cleaning process — sand and shell fragments removed, washed, sun-dried, sorted by size, then baled for export.
