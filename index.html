Direct-Sourcing Model

Most seaweed in Indonesia changes hands three or four times before it reaches a container, and the farmer's story gets lost along the way.

We buy straight from the farmers we work with, so every bag stays tied to the person who grew it.

Farm to container

Our Process

01

Farm

Grown on racks or long-lines suited to each species, on farms we've mapped and registered by GPS.

02

Harvest

Graded by our field team at the point of harvest, tagged with a batch ID tied to that farmer and plot.

03

Dry

Sun-dried on raised bamboo racks — never machine-dried, which preserves colour and gel strength.

04

Verify

Second inspection and lab testing at our consolidation point, checked against the batch record from harvest.

05

Ship

Container-ready packaging, with every bag traceable back to the farmer and farm plot it came from.

Export logistics

Farm to Port, Fully Traceable

Every shipment moves through the same documented chain, from the water to the vessel.

Stage 1–2

Harvest & Sun Drying

Cut and tagged at the farm, then sun-dried on raised bamboo racks — never machine-dried.

Stage 3–4

Sorting & Baling

Hand-sorted to remove foreign matter, lab-checked, then compressed and baled to shipping spec.

Stage 5

Port Shipment

Container-loaded and shipped via major Indonesian ports, including Surabaya and Jakarta, on FOB or CIF terms.

Quality control

Checked Before It Ever Leaves the Beach

  • Moisture-tested before packing, to keep shipments free of mould in transit
  • Hand-sorted — sand, shell fragments, and mixed species removed by hand
  • Lab-checked — gel strength, viscosity, and ash content confirmed before export
  • Sun-dried, not machine-dried — better colour and gel quality, the traditional way our farmers have always dried it
  • Container-ready packaging — matched to destination climate and shipping conditions
  • Full traceability — every bag tagged to the farmer, farm plot, and harvest week it came from
