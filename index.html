Our Story

Building Indonesia's First Fully Traceable Seaweed Supply Chain

SeaweedAsia started with a simple frustration: too many layers between the farmer who grows the seaweed and the buyer who needs it. Brokers add cost. Middlemen add uncertainty. And somewhere in between, quality control becomes someone else's problem.

Since 2023, we've built an export house that works directly with coastal farming cooperatives across the Indonesian archipelago, from Nias to Papua. No layers, no handoffs. Our own team grades the harvest, runs the lab tests, and manages the container all the way to the port.

What We Export

Five Species From Indonesian Waters

Each one traceable back to the farm bed it grew on. Every harvest gets a farmer ID, a GPS-mapped location, and a batch record before it's sun-dried, graded, and packed for export.

Carrageenan grade

Cottonii — Eucheuma cottonii

Our highest-volume species, grown on long-lines and prized for its kappa carrageenan yield. We dry it to a consistent 35–38% moisture and hand-sort every batch before it's baled.

• **Moisture:** 35–38% at packing

• **Foreign Matter:** Capped at 3–5%

• **MOQ:** 1 container

Iota carrageenan

Spinosum — Eucheuma spinosum

Grown alongside cottonii but processed separately for buyers who need iota carrageenan's softer, more elastic gel — common in dairy and confectionery work.

• **Moisture:** 12% or below

• **Foreign Matter:** Kept minimal through hand-sorting

• **MOQ:** 1 container

Agar grade

Gracilaria — Gracilaria spp.

Farmed in brackish ponds rather than open sea, giving farmers more control over water quality and resulting in a cleaner, lower-ash harvest for agar production.

• **Moisture:** Typically under 12%

• **Foreign Matter:** Minimal

• **MOQ:** 1 container

Whole-leaf

Sea Lettuce — Ulva lactuca

Wild-harvested from reef flats and shade-dried instead of sun-dried to hold on to its colour and mineral content — a growing pick for the health-food segment.

• **Drying:** Shade-dried, not sun-dried

• **Form:** Whole-leaf, dried

• **MOQ:** 1 container

Alginate grade

Sargassum — Sargassum spp.

Wild-collected along our sourcing coastline and processed for alginate buyers, with rising interest from biostimulant and agri-input manufacturers.

• **Moisture:** 15% or below

• **Foreign Matter:** Minimal after cleaning

• **MOQ:** 1 container

Our Process

From Farm Bed to Container

Checked before it ever leaves the beach.

1. Farm

Grown on racks or long-lines suited to each species, on farms we've mapped and registered by GPS.

2. Harvest

Graded by our field team at the point of harvest, tagged with a batch ID tied to that farmer and plot.

3. Dry

Sun-dried on raised bamboo racks — never machine-dried, which preserves colour and gel strength.

4. Verify

Second inspection and lab testing at our consolidation point, checked against the batch record from harvest.

5. Ship

Container-ready packaging, with every bag traceable back to the farmer and farm plot it came from.
