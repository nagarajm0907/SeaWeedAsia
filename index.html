Quality control

Checked before it ever leaves the beach

Field agents grade every batch at the point of harvest; a second inspection happens at our consolidation warehouse before loading.

Moisture-tested

Every lot is checked to sit within 35–38% moisture before packing, preventing mould in transit.

Hand-sorted

Sand, shell fragments, and other seaweed species are removed by hand before bailing.

Lab-verified

Independent labs confirm gel strength, viscosity, and ash content on every shipment batch.

Sun-dried, not machine-dried

Raised bamboo racks preserve colour and gel quality better than kiln-drying.

Container-ready packaging

Ventilated woven bags and moisture-barrier liners are matched to each destination climate.

Full traceability

Every bag is tagged back to its farming cooperative and harvest week.
