Traceable Seaweed, Direct From Indonesia

We work directly with coastal farmers across Indonesia, tracking every harvest from farm bed to container — no brokers, no blind sourcing.

SeaweedAsia is building Indonesia's first fully traceable seaweed supply chain — connecting farming communities to food, cosmetic, and hydrocolloid manufacturers with GPS-mapped, QR-verified sourcing on every shipment.

Direct-Sourcing Model

Most seaweed in Indonesia changes hands three or four times before it reaches a container, and the farmer's story gets lost along the way.

We buy straight from the farmers we work with, so every bag stays tied to the person who grew it.
