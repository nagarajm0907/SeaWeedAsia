Farming & Quality Control

From Ocean Cultivation to Export Shipment

1. Nursery & Cultivation

Farmers hand-tie seedling cuttings onto monofilament long-lines anchored in clean oceanic channels.

2. Selective Harvesting

Seaweed biomass is selectively harvested at peak growth maturity to preserve optimal gel extraction yields.

3. Solar Platform Drying

Drying is performed on raised bamboo racks to keep raw seaweed free from sand and coastal impurities.
