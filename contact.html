Common questions

FAQ

What are your payment terms?

Typically a 30% deposit via T/T upon order confirmation, with the remaining 70% due before shipment. Letters of Credit (L/C) can be arranged for larger orders.

What Incoterms do you offer?

FOB and CIF as standard, with CNF available on request.

What is your minimum order quantity?

1 container.

How long does production and shipping take?

Typically 14–30 days, depending on species and order timeline.

Do you provide samples before a full order?

Yes — request one through the form above. Depending on logistics, samples typically take up to 30 days to arrive.

Which ports do you ship from?

All major ports across Indonesia.
