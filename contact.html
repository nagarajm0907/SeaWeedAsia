Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our export terms, timelines, and logistics.

What are your payment terms? Terms

Typically a 30% deposit via T/T upon order confirmation, with the remaining 70% due before shipment. Letters of Credit (L/C) can be arranged for larger orders.

What Incoterms do you offer? Shipping

FOB and CIF as standard, with CNF available on request to fit your port delivery preferences.

What is your minimum order quantity? MOQ

1 standard container load, optimized for safe and cost-efficient international maritime transport.

How long does production and shipping take? Timeline

Typically 14–30 days total, precisely depending on the specific species requested and order volume.

Do you provide samples before a full order? Samples

Yes — you can request physical samples through our RFQ form. Standard logistica delivery times apply.

Which ports do you ship from? Ports

We manage direct export fulfillment from all major commercial ports across the Indonesian archipelago.
