Our Mission & Heritage

Bridging Indonesian Coastal Farmers to Global Industry.

SeaweedAsia was founded to professionalize and scale the supply chain of raw hydrocolloid seaweed from the Indonesian archipelago to global processors.

Empowering Coastal Farming Communities

Seaweed farming is the lifeblood of thousands of coastal villages across South Sulawesi, NTT, and NTB. By working directly with farming cooperatives, we eliminate unnecessary middle-tier markups and return fair values to local producers.

Our direct involvement guarantees strict quality control right at the drying tables, ensuring high gel strength, low moisture content, and contamination-free shipments.

Why Choose Us

Standardizing the Raw Marine Commodity Trade

Industrial buyers require consistency. Variable moisture percentages or high sand impurities lead to major operational losses during extraction. SeaweedAsia solves this with standardized solar platform drying and strict moisture capping before baling.
