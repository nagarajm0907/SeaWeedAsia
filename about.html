Our Mission & Base

Empowering Coastal Communities, Delivering Industrial Standards

SeaweedAsia connects local farming cooperatives in South Sulawesi and NTT directly to global supply chains. By guaranteeing fair purchasing rates and supplying standardized drying equipment, we help coastal communities thrive.

Our trade operations are managed out of Jakarta, securing consistent batch parameters, direct logistics, and reliable year-round fulfillment for international buyers.
