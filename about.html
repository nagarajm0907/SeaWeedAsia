Since 2023

Our Story

SeaweedAsia started with a simple frustration: too many layers between the farmer who grows the seaweed and the buyer who needs it.

Brokers add cost. Middlemen add uncertainty. And somewhere in between, quality control becomes someone else's problem.

Since 2023, we've built something different — an export house that works directly with coastal farming cooperatives across the Indonesian archipelago, from Nias to Papua. No layers, no handoffs. Our own team grades the harvest, runs the lab tests, and manages the container all the way to the port.

That direct model now moves an estimated ~50 containers a year, sourced from 32 cooperatives, to buyers in 5 countries — but the reason for it hasn't changed. Farmers get fair, dependable pricing and a partner who shows up season after season. Manufacturers get seaweed they can trace back to the exact farm bed it grew on.

Vision

Our Vision

To become the most trusted name in Indonesian seaweed export — recognized by global manufacturers for consistent quality, and by coastal farming communities for fair, dependable partnership.

Mission

Our Mission

To connect Indonesia's coastal farming communities with the world's food, cosmetic, and pharmaceutical manufacturers — building a supply chain where quality is verified, farmers are paid fairly, and every container can be traced back to the water it came from.
