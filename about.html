Our story

Built on the same tide lines our farmers have worked for generations.

SeaweedAsia started with a simple frustration: farmers along the archipelago were growing some of the best carrageenan-grade seaweed in the world, but were rarely the ones capturing its value. We set out to close that gap — grading fairly, paying on harvest day, and giving overseas buyers a direct line to the water instead of three layers of middlemen.

Every bag we export still has sand from the beach it dried on. That's the point — we never let the product get so far from the farm that we lose track of who grew it. — Nagaraj, Founder, SeaweedAsia

Our process, dive-log style

Four stages, each one adding a checkpoint — not a middleman — between the farm bed and your factory floor.

STAGE 01 — GROW LINES

Seeded on long-lines, 2–4 metres down

Farmers tie seed stock to monofilament long-lines anchored across shallow reef flats. Cottonii and spinosum mature in 45 days; gracilaria ponds run on a slightly longer cycle.

STAGE 02 — HARVEST & DRY

Hand-cut at dawn, dried the same day

Crews harvest by hand at low tide, then spread the wet seaweed across raised bamboo racks. Sun-drying over 2–3 days locks in gel strength better than any kiln.

STAGE 03 — GRADE & CONSOLIDATE

Sorted by hand, weighed on the beach

A field agent grades and weighs each farmer's harvest on-site, paying out same day. Batches are trucked to our regional warehouse for a second quality pass.

STAGE 04 — TEST & SHIP

Lab-verified, bagged, and containerised

Independent labs confirm moisture, gel strength, and ash content before we bag, palletise, and load — every shipment traceable back to the cooperative that grew it.

What we stand for

Three commitments we don't compromise on

FAIR

Same-day payment

Farmers are paid in full on the day they deliver — no consignment, no waiting on our export cycle to get paid for their harvest.

CLEAN

Sustainable density

We cap long-line density per hectare with our cooperatives to protect reef health and keep yields stable season after season.

CLEAR

Radical traceability

Every export document links back to a named cooperative and harvest week — no blended, unsourced lots, ever.
